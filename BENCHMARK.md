# AI-Checker — Benchmark Corpus Specification

> **Status:** Draft v1 for PO/BA approval · **Owner:** PO/BA + Interns · **Task:** TASKS.md **D4** · **Phase:** 1 (spec) → 2 (execution)
>
> This document defines *how we will measure detection quality*. It is a specification, not a results report — no numbers here are measurements until Phase 2 executes the protocol against the built corpus.

---

## 1. Purpose

Every provider claims accuracy numbers we cannot verify, and `aiProbability` arrives on different scales per vendor. Before AI-Checker makes any accuracy claim — or tunes ensemble weights, verdict thresholds, or provider priority — it needs a **single, reusable benchmark** that:

1. Measures each provider's per-modality accuracy **on the same inputs** (apples-to-apples).
2. Supplies the calibration data for `toVerdict()` bucket tuning (currently 0–34 / 35–65 / 66–100, set by judgment rather than evidence).
3. Becomes the regression gate for provider swaps and ensemble scoring (ROADMAP Phase 3).

**Out of scope for v1:** live-production evaluation, adversarial red-teaming beyond the robustness slice, and multi-language coverage beyond English + Bangla.

---

## 2. Corpus Composition

| Modality | Slice | Target size (v1) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text | Human-written | 250 samples | ≥3 distinct authors per source domain |
| Text | AI-generated | 250 samples | Spread across ≥3 generators and ≥3 prompt styles |
| Text | Mixed/hybrid | 50 samples | Human-edited AI drafts and AI-polished human drafts |
| Image | Human (camera) | 200 samples | Mixed devices; EXIF retained where possible |
| Image | AI-generated | 200 samples | Spread across ≥3 generators (diffusion + GAN families) |
| Image | AI-edited human | 50 samples | Inpainting / generative-fill hybrids |
| Audio | Human (real recording) | 150 samples | Speech; varied mics and rooms |
| Audio | AI-generated speech | 150 samples | TTS across ≥3 systems |
| Audio | AI music | 50 samples | Kept as a separate labeled subset |
| Video | Human (camera) | 100 samples | Same camera pool as the image corpus where possible |
| Video | AI-generated | 100 samples | Spread across ≥2 generators |
| **Total** | | **~1,550 samples** | v1 floor: **800** (see §6) |

### Sources

- **Human slices:** self-recorded/created by the team (preferred — licensing-clean and provably human), plus permissively-licensed public datasets (e.g. common TTS-detection research sets) with license recorded per item.
- **AI slices:** generated by the team with documented model + prompt/recording settings per item.
- **Provenance rule:** every sample carries `source`, `generator_or_author`, `tool_version`, `creation_date`, `license`. No provenance → not in the corpus.

---

## 3. Labeling Rules & Adjudication

- Ground truth is **binary per sample** (`human` / `ai`), with the mixed/hybrid slices labeled by **majority-origin** and flagged `hybrid`.
- **Two-pass labeling:** two labelers annotate independently; disagreements go to a third reviewer (PO/BA) for adjudication. Adjudicated samples keep an `adjudicated: true` flag.
- Labelers never label slices they personally created.
- Ambiguous-by-construction items (e.g. "AI-generated but heavily human-edited") belong to the hybrid slices, not the clean ones.

---

## 4. Evaluation Protocol

1. **Freeze a manifest** (`manifest.jsonl`: sample id, modality, label, subgroup, provenance). The manifest is versioned; corpus updates never mutate a published version.
2. Run each provider over the manifest with the **same adapter code path the app uses** (no special-casing).
3. Record raw `aiProbability` per sample — never verdicts only.
4. **Batch discipline:** respect each provider's free-tier quota; run in batches with recorded timestamps so a quota-throttled run can be detected and discarded.
5. A provider that errors on a sample records `error` — errors are **reported, not silently dropped** (an adapter that fails on 20% of inputs is not 80% accurate on the rest).

---

## 5. Metrics (per provider × modality)

| Metric | Definition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **ROC-AUC** | Primary quality metric, threshold-free | Comparable across vendors with different score scales |
| **Accuracy @ 0.5** | Threshold on the normalized score | Baseline sanity number |
| **Accuracy @ tuned** | Threshold tuned on a held-out split | The number that matters for production |
| **FPR @ 95% TPR** | False-accusation rate at high recall | The user-harm metric — falsely flagging human work is the costly error |
| **Calibration (ECE)** | Expected calibration error of `aiProbability` | Determines whether scores can drive `toVerdict()` buckets directly |
| **Error rate** | Adapter failures per provider | Failover-chain health signal |
| **Latency (p50/p95)** | End-to-end adapter time | Feeds provider priority ordering |

**Verdict-bucket validation:** buckets are evidence-based only when the calibrated score distributions of human vs AI populations separate cleanly. Until then, the README's "detection signal, not proof" disclaimer governs all output.

---

## 6. Acceptance Thresholds & Cadence

- **Corpus v1 floor:** 800 samples (scaled-down per-modality ratios preserved). Below the floor, results are labeled *indicative*, never quoted as accuracy claims.
- **Adoption bar:** a provider enters the fallback chain, or gets re-prioritized, only with a frozen-manifest run showing ROC-AUC ≥ the incumbent's − 0.02.
- **Regression gate (Phase 3):** any change to verdict thresholds, provider order, or ensemble weights must show **no FPR@95%TPR regression** on the current frozen manifest.
- **Cadence:** full re-run quarterly, or on any provider/model-change event, whichever comes first.

---

## 7. Fairness & Robustness Subgroups

Results are always reported **per subgroup**, never only in aggregate:

- Text: Bangla vs English; short (<100 words) vs long
- Image: photographic vs illustration styles
- Audio: clean vs noisy recordings; music vs speech
- Cross-cutting: hybrid slices reported separately from clean slices

Known industry failure mode to watch: detectors systematically misclassifying non-native or low-resource-language human text as AI. The Bangla subgroup exists specifically to catch this in our own primary market.

---

## 8. Governance

- **Owner:** PO/BA (spec, adjudication) + Interns (collection, labeling, scripts) per the ROADMAP §2 matrix.
- **Storage:** corpus artifacts live outside the repo (license hygiene); the repo carries this spec, the manifest schema, and the runner scripts when they exist.
- **Licensing:** every sample's license recorded at collection; no sample whose license forbids evaluation use.
- **Changes to this spec** require a PR reviewed by the Tech Lead and approved by the PO/BA.
